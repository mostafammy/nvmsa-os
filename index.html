Grounded in El Kharga, connected across Egypt

New Valley Medical Students' Association (NVMSA) represents medical students at New Valley University, pairing hands-on medical education with community service tailored to Egypt's Western Desert.

Our emblem joins two silhouettes: the limestone gateway of Hibis Temple standing in El Kharga Oasis, and a living cardiac trace rising between its columns. Together they reflect physicians in training who stay rooted in their governorate while advancing modern clinical standards.

Through IFMSA-Egypt standing committees and specialized support divisions, NVMSA equips students to run school and campus health campaigns, organize scientific conferences, practice clinical reasoning, and represent New Valley at national assemblies.

Community health in the oasis

Physical vital-signs checks, first-aid booths, school awareness visits, and digital campaigns reaching tens of thousands across New Valley and partner governorates.

Clinical and research capacity

Structured peer training in clinical reasoning, curriculum quality, research methodology, project management, and media production.

Inter-governorate collaboration

Joint campaigns with sister local committees from Aswan, Luxor, Minya, Assiut, Suez, Menofia, Benha, Al-Azhar, MUST, and Horus.

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers for New Valley medical students, prospective volunteers, and external partners.

How do I join NVMSA as a member or volunteer?

Recruitment waves and volunteer calls are announced on our official Facebook, Instagram, and Linktree channels rather than through a permanent single form. Follow our channels or message the Secretary General on WhatsApp for the next intake window.

Who can join NVMSA?

Undergraduate medical students at the Faculty of Medicine, New Valley University can join Standing Committees and Support Divisions. Community partners, alumni, and local sponsors can collaborate on public health and conference initiatives.

How are official emails and WhatsApp contacts organized?

Each of the 13 official seats holds a dedicated @gmail.com role address (such as president.nvmsa@gmail.com or psdd.nvmsa@gmail.com) so continuity survives handovers. Direct WhatsApp links are listed for officials with published numbers.

What is NVMSA OS at /nvmsaOs?

NVMSA OS is our internal operations platform used by the Executive Board, Standing Committee Officers, and Support Division Directors to book the single daily publication slot without conflicts.

Can hospitals, NGOs, or local businesses sponsor NVMSA?

Yes. Contact our Treasurer (treasurer.nvmsa@gmail.com) or Project Support Division Director (psdd.nvmsa@gmail.com / +20 102 724 6280) to review event or merchandise sponsorship tiers.

Where can I view older term archives before 2025/2026?

The 2025/2026 End-of-Term Report ("No Hero Without Wounds") is fully published in the History section above. Earlier term sheets (2024/2025 and 2023/2024) are staged as archive slots awaiting digitized handover files.
